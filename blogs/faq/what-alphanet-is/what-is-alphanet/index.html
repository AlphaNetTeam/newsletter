What Is AlphaNet?

FAQ · WHAT ALPHANET IS2 MIN READ

AlphaNet is a quantitative AI futures trading platform that brings strategy research, automated trading, risk controls and algorithmic execution into one system. Its purpose is to make a systematic trading stack accessible through a wallet-connected interface.

Two ways to use the platform

Standard Mode keeps trading decisions with the user and offers execution assistance. Autopilot runs a strategy chosen by the user; it does not automatically choose the whole portfolio.

More than a prediction model

A market forecast alone is not a complete trading system. The same forecast can produce different results depending on position size, entry and exit logic, transaction costs and the conditions in which it is used.

AlphaNet’s published research describes a hybrid approach combining systematic factors with models trained on structured market data. The model’s role is part of a wider process, rather than a chat response being treated as a finished trading instruction.

The central distinction is between choosing the portfolio and managing each trade. Users retain the former decision; a deployed systematic strategy handles the latter within its design. Automation does not remove market risk, and historical performance is not a promise of future returns.
