SOTA Algo Execution Engine

Having a good trading signal is only half the battle. The other half — often overlooked by retail traders — is execution quality. Every basis point lost to slippage compounds over thousands of trades.

AlphaNet's State-of-the-Art (SOTA) algo execution engine is a deep-learning-based order execution system that dynamically breaks large orders into smaller chunks for optimal execution. The engine considers:

  • Price drift risk: How likely is the price to move against the order during execution?
  • Directional price prediction: Where is the price heading in the short term?
  • Liquidity analysis: Where is the depth in the orderbook?

As an order plays out, the engine dynamically speeds up or slows down execution to optimize for the best average fill price. The current implementation features Dynamic TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) — a deep-learning-based algorithm that operates under a fixed time constraint and updates execution policy in real-time based on order size, orderbook, price, volume, and market conditions.

This is the same class of execution technology used by institutional trading desks — now available to anyone with a wallet.

AlphaNet's Trading Instruments: Perpetual Futures on DEXs

What is a Perp DEX?

A perpetual futures DEX is a decentralized exchange that offers perpetual futures contracts — derivative instruments that allow traders to speculate on the price direction of an asset without actually owning it, and without an expiration date.

  • Non-custodial: You retain control of your funds in your own wallet
  • Transparent: All trades and positions are verifiable on the blockchain
  • Permissionless: No KYC, no account creation, no geographic restrictions
  • Composable: Positions and strategies can interact with other DeFi protocols

Why Perpetual Futures?

  1. Leverage without borrowing: Perps allow leveraged exposure without needing to borrow funds
  2. Both directions: Traders can go long or short
  3. Funding rate arbitrage: The funding rate mechanism creates additional alpha opportunities
  4. Deep liquidity: Perp markets on major DEXs often have deeper liquidity than spot markets
  5. 24/7 markets: Crypto perps never close
