WHY POINTS

A different kind of
points system.

Most platforms use points to bootstrap hype ahead of a token launch — value purely speculative. AlphaNet Points are a claim on real, generated income: trading fees from AI alpha strategies. A TGE is an option, not a necessity — the system is economically meaningful regardless.

/01

Revenue-anchored

Points claim a share of real platform income — fees on every trade via the dynamic multi-factor fee engine — not speculative token value.

/02

Deployment-based

Earned through capital commitment, not engagement farming. No rewards for logins or clicks — points require actual skin in the game.

/03

Multi-participant

Users, guild leaders and creators each have aligned earning paths — calibrated to the nature and magnitude of their contribution.

/04

Evolving

Guild multipliers, promotional bonuses and taper parameters adjust per epoch — built-in adaptability for long-term sustainability.

DISTRIBUTION

Two phases. Sustained
engagement wins.

Platform fees are not yet activated — points accumulate now in a pre-revenue phase and become claimable against the future distribution pool: a dedicated share of trading fee revenue. Deploying early builds your balance before the pool exists.

S0

Stage Zero — initial rewards

Once fees activate, a portion of trading fee revenue flows into the distribution pool, allocated proportionally to total points accumulated. Transparent, proportional, verifiable.

PROPORTIONAL TO TOTAL POINTS
S1

Stage One — epoch-based taper

Once the taper activates, reward weighting steps monthly from lifetime totals toward the most recent epoch. Sustained engagement always matters.

WEIGHTED TOWARD RECENT EPOCHS
REWARD WEIGHT OVER TIME LIFETIME TOTAL PTS RECENT EPOCH PTS
01

Start — lifetime total dominates. When fees switch on, your reward share is based almost entirely (~100%) on your all-time points total.

02

Monthly steps toward the recent epoch. Each month the weighting shifts one step away from lifetime totals, toward points earned in the most recent epoch.

03

Steady state — ~25% / ~75%. Lifetime total settles at ~25% of the weight and the recent epoch carries ~75% — even the largest holders must keep participating.
